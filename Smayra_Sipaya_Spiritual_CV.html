✦ Profile & Reading Philosophy

My relationship with tarot began with a fascination for the stories, visual motifs, and living archetypes held within its imagery. What began as a personal exploration of the cards has matured into a disciplined, ongoing inquiry into how symbolism, psychology, and intuition intersect to illuminate the human condition.

I deliberately approach tarot as more than a predictive or fortune-telling device. To me, it is an intricate symbolic language—a mirror of emotional architectures, behavioral patterns, and unconscious narratives that helps individuals examine their circumstances with clarity and agency. My practice bridges traditional Rider–Waite-Smith iconography with Carl Jung's analytical psychology (the shadow, the anima/animus, individuation) and Hermetic philosophy. Through private consultations, campus readings, and independent writing, my intention is to provide grounded, intellectually honest, and compassionate guidance in a confidential, non-judgmental space.

✦ Areas of Inquiry & Specialisation

  • Tarot Studies & Spread Dynamics: Deep study of the Rider–Waite tradition, the symbolic architecture of the Major and Minor Arcana, multi-card contextual relationships, and specialized spread geometry (e.g., Celtic Cross, temporal mirrors, custom archetypal blueprints).
  • Analytical & Archetypal Psychology: Practical application of Jungian theory—exploring archetypes, shadow integration, the collective unconscious, active imagination, and personal individuation through card symbolism.
  • Esoteric & Hermetic Philosophy: Independent research into Hermetic principles (correspondence, polarity, rhythm), elemental associations (Fire, Water, Air, Earth), sacred geometry, and esoteric correspondences.
  • Intuitive & Symbolic Synthesis: Harmonizing classical iconography with non-linear intuitive perception, contextual nuances, and real-time client dialogue rather than reciting rigid, textbook definitions.
  • Reflective & Contemplative Practices: Employing tarot as an introspective medium for self-exploration, reflective journaling, recognizing unconscious habits, and navigating complex life transitions.
